Privacy

Last updated July 29, 2026

LaidIn is a pre-launch software product operated by Jessica L. Rutter. This page describes, plainly, what this website collects and how it is used.

What we collect

If you apply for founding access, we collect the information you enter in the form: your email address and the business details you provide, such as your name, company, and role. That information is stored with Kit, our email service provider, and is used to contact you about LaidIn.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the page, such as which pages are viewed and where visitors arrive from. Google Analytics may set cookies for that purpose.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties beyond the service providers named above, which we use to operate this site and our mailing list.

Email

Before launch we send occasional US pricing insights and product previews. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect immediately.

Your choices

To unsubscribe, use the link in any email. To have your information corrected or deleted, write to contact@thelaidin.com and we will take care of it.
