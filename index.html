The problem

Most wine and spirits brands and importers discover their real margin only after losing money, and that pain compounds in every state you sell, every channel you add, and every deal you say yes to. One mispriced SKU can quietly erase a brand's profit in an entire state.

A two dollar per case mistake does not sound dangerous. Apply it to five SKUs selling 1,000 cases each month, and it becomes $10,000 in lost margin every month. That is $120,000 a year, gone before the economics ever became visible.

The solution

LaidIn turns FOB, freight, state taxes, and distributor allowances into a clear picture of price lanes, blended margins, and US market-entry scenarios, so wineries, distilleries, importers, and distributors can make confident decisions before they ship a single case.

Built for the three-tier US market

One FOB can produce entirely different economics from state to state. Freight, excise taxes, control-state structures, distributor allowances, channel margins, and your target shelf position all change what your brand can charge, and what you keep. LaidIn puts those variables in one place, so you can compare scenarios before committing inventory or entering a new state.
